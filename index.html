Selected work

Three real tasks, shown from problem to result.

Product Listing

The problem
The Shopify product listing was blank. It had no title, description, images, price or metafields.
What I did
I added the product as "Aquaflask". I wrote the description, uploaded the images, set the price and stock, and filled in the metafields and variants.
The result
A complete, organized listing with full information for buyers.

Product Optimization

The problem
Search engines and shoppers could not find the store or its product images. The meta descriptions, page titles and image alt text were missing.
What I did
I set up the store's SEO and social sharing. I wrote a keyword-rich meta title and description for the Aqua Flask 32oz Cobalt Blue bottle. I added descriptive alt text to the product images.
The result
An optimized listing that is easier to find in search and more accessible to screen reader users.

Customer Service

The problem
Customers wrote in about delivery delays, damaged items and changes to orders. Each one needed a quick, professional reply.
What I did
I wrote polite, caring replies in Gmail. I started a shipping trace and a priority replacement for a late order. I asked for a photo of a leaked item to process a refund. I confirmed an order change made before the cutoff.
The result
Clear, reassuring replies that resolve tickets quickly and keep customers' trust in the brand.
